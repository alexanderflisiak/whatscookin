## 2024-04-17 - Add ARIA Labels and Focus Indicators to Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Found a pattern of icon-only interactive elements (like the "Surprise Me" button and ingredient removal trash can) missing proper `aria-label`s, rendering them completely inaccessible to screen readers. Additionally, toggle buttons (like "Lazy Mode") lacked the required `aria-pressed` attribute to communicate their state to assistive technologies, and keyboard navigation was difficult without clear `focus-visible` indicators on these elements.
**Action:** When adding new icon-only buttons or interactive elements in the codebase, always ensure `aria-label` is provided to give context, use `aria-pressed` for toggle states, and apply `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-stone-400 focus-visible:outline-none` classes to maintain consistent and accessible keyboard navigation.
