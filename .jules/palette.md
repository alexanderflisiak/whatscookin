## 2025-04-02 - Custom Toggle Buttons Require `aria-pressed` and `focus-visible`
**Learning:** Custom interactive elements (like icon-only buttons or stateful toggles) in this app frequently lacked essential accessibility attributes (`aria-label`, `aria-pressed`) and visible focus states for keyboard navigation (`focus-visible`). This makes it difficult for screen reader users to understand their purpose or state, and for keyboard-only users to navigate the interface.
**Action:** When creating or reviewing custom buttons and toggles, always ensure they have an `aria-label` (if icon-only), `aria-pressed` (if stateful), and distinct `focus-visible` utility classes (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-stone-400 focus-visible:outline-none`) to provide clear visual feedback during keyboard navigation.
